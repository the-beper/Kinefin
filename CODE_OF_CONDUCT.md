# Code of conduct

Kinefin follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). It applies to the issues, pull requests and every other space of this repository, and to anyone who speaks for the project in public.

Be respectful, assume good faith and keep feedback on the work. Do not harass, insult, discriminate or publish other people's private information.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately to the maintainer through the repository's [private reporting form](https://github.com/the-beper/Kinefin/security/advisories/new) (put "Conduct" in the title), the only private channel of this repository. Only the maintainer reads the report. A comment that breaks the rules can also be reported to GitHub with the "Report content" entry of its menu.

## Enforcement

The maintainer can edit or delete comments, close or lock threads, and block people from the repository, following the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant.
